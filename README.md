# COSMOS ORIGIN

An open-source scientific knowledge project exploring the origin,
evolution, and large-scale history of the Universe.

## About

COSMOS ORIGIN is an educational and research-oriented project that
organizes scientific knowledge about the Universe into a structured,
readable, and interactive format.

The project covers the history of the Universe from its earliest
known stages through the formation of particles, atoms, stars,
galaxies, planets, and eventually life.

The project clearly separates:

- Observational evidence
- Established scientific models
- Current scientific hypotheses
- Open questions
- Historical ideas that have been replaced or rejected

## Scientific Philosophy

COSMOS ORIGIN does not present speculation as established fact.

Every major claim should be classified according to the strength
of the available evidence.

Possible evidence levels include:

- Established
- Strong observational support
- Supported model
- Active research
- Hypothesis
- Unknown
